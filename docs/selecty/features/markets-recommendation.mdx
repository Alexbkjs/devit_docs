The Markets Recommendation feature displays a banner to first-time visitors, suggesting they switch to their local language and currency based on their location and browser preferences. This creates a personalized shopping experience without forcing automatic changes.

<Tip>
Looking for information about "banners" or "popups"? Check our [Glossary](/selecty/support/glossary) for commonly used feature names and terminology.
</Tip>

## How It Works

When someone visits your store, Selecty automatically:

1. **Detects their location** using IP geolocation
2. **Identifies their language preference** from browser settings
3. **Displays a recommendation banner** suggesting the most relevant language and currency
4. **Handles subsequent visits** based on your [Display Settings](#display-settings) configuration

<Info>
How the banner behaves on subsequent visits depends on your display option. See [Common Scenarios](#common-scenarios) to find the right option for your store.
</Info>

## Common Scenarios

Not sure which display option to use? Find your use case below:

| What you want | Display option |
|--------------|----------------|
| Show banner on first visit, remember choice and auto-switch for returning visitors | [Remember choice](#remember-choice-first-visit-banner-automatic-switching-for-returning-visitors) |
| Show banner only when needed, re-show if customer manually changes market | [Display when necessary](#display-when-necessary) (Recommended) |
| Force automatic switching without giving users a choice | [Forced recommendations](#forced-recommendations) |
| Show banner once and never again, regardless of future visits | [Display once](#display-once) |
| Show banner to every first-time visitor, even those in the correct market | [Display for everyone](#display-for-everyone) |

## Why Use Recommendations Instead of Auto-Redirect?

Google and other search engines recommend against automatic redirects for multilingual websites because:

- **SEO Impact** - Automatic redirects can interfere with proper indexing of multilingual content
- **User Control** - Recommendations give users choice rather than forcing changes
- **Better UX** - Visitors can review the suggestion before accepting

<Tip>
We strongly recommend using recommendation banners instead of automatic redirects. See our [SEO guide](/advanced/seo) and [Markets Redirect](/features/markets-redirect) documentation for more details.
</Tip>

## Detection Methods

### Language Detection

Selecty detects language preferences from the visitor's browser settings.

**How Browser Language Works:**

Browsers maintain a list of preferred languages that you can configure in settings. The first language in this list is what Selecty uses for recommendations.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Chrome">
    1. Go to **Settings** → **Languages**
    2. View your preferred languages list
    3. The top language is your primary preference
  </Tab>

  <Tab title="Firefox">
    1. Go to **Settings**
    2. Scroll to **Language** section
    3. Check your preferred languages order
  </Tab>

  <Tab title="Safari">
    1. Go to **Preferences** → **General**
    2. Check **Preferred languages**
    3. Language order determines priority
  </Tab>

  <Tab title="Edge">
    1. Go to **Settings** → **Languages**
    2. View preferred languages
    3. Top language is used for detection
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

<Note>
Browser language and display language can be different. For example, you might use German as your browser display language but have English set as your preferred content language.
</Note>

### Country and Currency Detection

Country and currency are detected using **IP geolocation**.

**How It Works:**

1. Your Internet Service Provider (ISP) assigns you an IP address
2. Selecty uses this IP to determine your country of residence
3. Based on the country, Selecty suggests the local currency

<Warning>
IP geolocation is generally accurate but not perfect. VPNs, proxies, and some corporate networks may show incorrect locations.
</Warning>

## Creating a Recommendation Banner

<Steps>
  <Step title="Access Markets Recommendations">
    In the Selecty app, scroll to **Markets Recommendations** and click **Customize**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Choose Resource">
    Select what to recommend:

    - **Language** - Suggest visitor's preferred language
    - **Currency** - Suggest local currency based on location
    - **Language & Currency** - Recommend both together
    - **Market** - Suggest appropriate market for their region
  </Step>

  <Step title="Select Banner Type">
    Choose your banner layout:

    - **Top Bar** - Horizontal banner across the top
    - **Bottom Bar** - Banner at the bottom of the page
    - **Modal** - Center popup overlay
    - **Corner Notification** - Small banner in page corner
    - **Slide-in** - Banner that slides from the side
  </Step>

  <Step title="Pick Animation Style">
    Select entry animation:

    - Slide in
    - Fade in
    - Bounce
    - Scale
    - None (instant display)
  </Step>

  <Step title="Customize Theme & Text">
    In the **Theme** tab, customize:

    - **Colors** - Background, text, and button colors
    - **Message Text** - Customize the recommendation message
    - **Button Labels** - "Yes, switch" and "No, thanks" text
    - **Fonts** - Typography options
    - **Icons** - Show flags or currency symbols
  </Step>

  <Step title="Configure Behavior">
    Set banner behavior:

    - **Display duration** - How long before auto-dismiss
    - **Cookie lifetime** - How long to remember user preference
    - **Show on specific pages** - Target specific URLs or page types
    - **Exclude pages** - Pages where banner shouldn't appear
  </Step>

  <Step title="Save and Enable">
    Click **Save** in the upper right corner to activate your banner.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Testing Your Banner

To test your recommendation banner:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Use a VPN">
    Enable a VPN to simulate visiting from a different country. Make sure:
    - The VPN country is different from your primary market
    - The VPN is active in incognito mode if using a browser extension
  </Step>

  <Step title="Open Incognito/Private Window">
    Use incognito mode to simulate a first-time visitor:
    - **Chrome:** Ctrl+Shift+N (Windows) or ⌘+Shift+N (Mac)
    - **Firefox:** Ctrl+Shift+P or ⌘+Shift+P
    - **Safari:** ⌘+Shift+N
  </Step>

  <Step title="Visit Your Store">
    Navigate to your store in the incognito window. The banner should appear if:
    - Your location (via VPN) matches a different market
    - The banner is enabled in Selecty
    - You haven't dismissed it before in this browser
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Info>
For detailed testing instructions, see our [Testing Geolocation](/advanced/testing-geolocation) guide.
</Info>

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Banner not appearing">
    **Check these settings:**
    - Banner is enabled in Selecty app
    - You're testing in incognito mode
    - VPN is set to a different market
    - Shopify's auto-redirect is disabled (Settings → Markets → Preferences)
    - Markets Redirect is disabled in Selecty app
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Wrong language suggested">
    The language is based on browser settings, not location. Check your browser's preferred languages list in settings.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Wrong currency/country suggested">
    This uses IP geolocation. VPNs, proxies, and some networks may show incorrect locations. Try a different VPN server.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Banner appears every time">
    Clear your browser cookies and cache, then test again. The banner should only appear once per unique visitor.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Display Settings

The Display settings control when your recommendation banner appears to visitors. These options help you balance providing helpful suggestions with respecting customer preferences and browsing patterns.

### Accessing Display Settings

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Markets Recommendations">
    In the Selecty app, navigate to **Markets Recommendations** and click **Customize**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Go to Settings Tab">
    Click on the **Settings** tab at the top of the customization panel.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Find Display Options">
    The **Display** dropdown contains five behavior options for controlling when banners appear.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Display Options

#### Display when necessary

**Recommended for most stores**

Shows recommendations when visitors are browsing with the wrong language, currency, or country, and remembers their choice. Recommendations will only appear again if the customer changes their language, currency, or country afterwards.

**How it works:**

1. Visitor arrives from a country that doesn't match their current market
2. Banner appears suggesting the appropriate market
3. Customer makes a choice (accept or dismiss)
4. Selecty remembers this choice
5. Banner won't appear again unless the customer manually switches to a different language, currency, or country

**Best for:**
- Stores that want to be helpful without being pushy
- Respecting customer choices while staying responsive to changes
- Balancing user experience with conversion optimization

**Example scenario:**
> A visitor from France arrives at your UK store (English/GBP). The banner suggests switching to French/EUR. They dismiss it. The banner won't appear again during their session or future visits, unless they manually switch to Spanish or another market first—then the banner will suggest French/EUR again.

<Tip>
This is the recommended option for most stores as it provides helpful suggestions without repeatedly interrupting visitors who have already made their preference clear.
</Tip>

#### Forced recommendations

Shows recommendations when visitors are browsing with a wrong language, currency, or country with only one recommended option, and always switches to it automatically afterwards.

**How it works:**

1. Visitor arrives at your store from a different market
2. Banner appears with only the recommended option (no choice to decline)
3. After a short delay, automatically switches to the recommended market
4. Applies to every visit where the market doesn't match location/language

**Best for:**
- Stores with strict regional requirements (legal, licensing)
- B2B stores where correct market selection is critical
- Situations where wrong market selection causes significant issues

**Important considerations:**

<Warning>
Forced recommendations automatically redirect users without giving them a choice. This can negatively impact SEO and user experience. Only use this option if you have a specific business requirement for automatic market switching.
</Warning>

**Example scenario:**
> A visitor from Germany arrives at your US store. The banner appears showing only the German market option. After 3 seconds, they're automatically redirected to the German market. This happens on every visit.

#### Remember choice (First visit banner, automatic switching for returning visitors)

Show a recommendation banner on the customer's first visit, remember their preference, and automatically switch for subsequent visits without showing the banner again. This is ideal for stores with returning customers who want a seamless experience after the initial recommendation.

**How it works:**

1. First visit: Banner appears with recommendation
2. Customer accepts or dismisses
3. Selecty remembers this choice
4. On future visits from wrong market: Automatically switches based on remembered preference
5. No banner appears on subsequent visits

**Best for:**
- Stores with returning customers
- Reducing banner fatigue while maintaining personalization
- Streamlined experience for repeat visitors
- When you want to show a pop-up once and remember the selection for future visits

**Example scenario:**
> First visit: German visitor sees banner suggesting German market and accepts. Future visits: If they arrive at the US store again, they're automatically switched to German market without seeing the banner.

<Note>
This option relies on browser cookies to remember preferences. If customers clear cookies or visit from a different device/browser, they'll see the recommendation banner again.
</Note>

#### Display once

Show recommendations only once if needed and prevent displaying in the future.

**How it works:**

1. Banner appears on first visit (if market doesn't match location/language)
2. Customer makes their choice
3. Banner never appears again, regardless of:
   - Future visits
   - Market changes
   - Different countries or languages

**Best for:**
- Minimal interruption approach
- Stores where recommendation is informational only
- Markets where visitors frequently travel or use VPNs

**Example scenario:**
> A visitor from Canada arrives at your US store and dismisses the Canadian market suggestion. They'll never see the banner again, even if they manually switch to the UK market and visit again next week.

<Warning>
This option shows the banner only once ever. Even if a customer's needs change over time, they won't see recommendations again. Use this carefully.
</Warning>

#### Display for everyone

Show recommendations to every new visitor, regardless of the visitor's country, currency, or browser language.

**How it works:**

1. Every first-time visitor sees the banner
2. Recommendation is based on detected location and language
3. Even visitors from your primary market see suggestions
4. Appears once per unique visitor

**Best for:**
- Stores wanting maximum awareness of market options
- New stores building market awareness
- Testing recommendation banner effectiveness

**Example scenario:**
> All visitors see the banner on their first visit, including those already in the correct market. A US visitor to your US store might see "Continue shopping in English (USD)" as a confirmation rather than a suggestion to switch.

<Info>
This option maximizes banner visibility but may feel unnecessary to visitors who are already in the correct market. Consider whether the added awareness is worth the potential interruption.
</Info>

### Display Settings Best Practices

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Start with 'Display when necessary'">
    This option provides the best balance between helpfulness and user experience for most stores. You can adjust based on analytics and customer feedback.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Avoid forced recommendations unless required">
    Automatic redirects can frustrate users and harm SEO. Only use "Forced recommendations" if you have a specific business need that outweighs these concerns.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Monitor analytics">
    Track banner interaction rates to see if your display option is working effectively:
    - How many visitors see the banner
    - Acceptance vs. dismissal rates
    - Impact on conversion rates by market
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Consider cookie lifetime">
    The display option works in conjunction with cookie settings. Ensure your cookie lifetime matches your display strategy (e.g., longer cookies for "Remember choice", shorter for "Display when necessary").
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Combine with manual selectors">
    Always provide manual language/currency selectors so users can change markets even if they've dismissed the recommendation banner.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

### Display Settings FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How do I show a banner once and remember the choice for returning visitors?">
    Use the **Remember choice** display option. This shows the recommendation banner on the first visit, remembers the customer's decision, and automatically applies their preference on future visits without displaying the banner again.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What happens if I change the display option?">
    Changing the display option affects future visitor interactions. Existing cookies and remembered preferences remain until they expire or are cleared.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Do display options affect SEO?">
    "Display when necessary", "Remember choice", "Display once", and "Display for everyone" don't impact SEO because they show recommendations rather than forcing redirects. "Forced recommendations" can impact SEO similar to automatic redirects.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I exclude certain countries from recommendations?">
    Yes, you can configure market and country exclusions in other settings sections. The display option controls the behavior pattern, while other settings control which markets and countries trigger recommendations.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How long do preferences last?">
    Preference duration depends on your cookie lifetime settings, which you can configure in the Settings section. Default is typically 30 days.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
