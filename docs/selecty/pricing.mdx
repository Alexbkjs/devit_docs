Selecty offers a feature-rich free plan plus premium tiers for stores that need advanced customization and multi-store capabilities.

<Note>
All charges are billed in USD. Recurring and usage-based charges are billed every 30 days.
</Note>

## Pricing Plans

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Free" icon="gift">

    Perfect for most stores and includes all essential features.

    **Pricing**: **Free forever**

    - **All selector types** including Wheel, Modal, and advanced styles
    - **Automatic redirects** based on geolocation

    - **Advanced support** with faster response times
    - **More customization** options for branding

    **Best for:** Growing stores wanting to automate market switching and provide enhanced user experience
  </Tab>

    For agencies and stores managing multiple storefronts.

    **Pricing**: **\$21.99/month** or **\$197.91/year** (save 25%)

    **What's included:**
    - All Professional plan features
    - Titles and icons customization
    - Connect and manage multiple stores
    - Custom resources (third-party integrations)
    - Custom theme styles
    - Advanced customization tools
    - Advanced support
    - 7-day free trial

    **🔼 Upgrade from Professional:**
    - **Multiple stores connection** from one dashboard
    - **Full customization** of titles and icons
    - **Custom resources** for external platforms and third-party links
    - **Agency-ready** features for managing client stores

    **Best for:** Agencies, multi-brand operations, or stores requiring complete customization control
  </Tab>

## Choosing the Right Plan

Not sure which plan fits your needs? Here's our recommendation:

- **Free**: Most stores - includes all essential features for currency, language, and market switching with basic support
- **Professional**: Stores wanting all selector types, automatic market redirects, visibility settings, and advanced support
- **Premium**: Agencies, brands with multiple stores, or stores needing custom resources and complete customization control

<Tip>
Start with the Free plan to test Selecty. You can upgrade anytime if you need advanced features like automatic redirects or multi-store management.
</Tip>

## Frequently Asked Questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Is the free plan really unlimited?">
    Yes! The free plan has no limits on:
    - Number of selectors
    - Currencies, languages, or markets
    - Store traffic

    You can use Selecty free forever without any usage restrictions.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What selector types are included in each plan?">
    **Free plan** includes Dropdown and Inline selector types, which cover the essential needs for most stores.

    **Professional and Premium plans** include all selector types: Wheel, Modal, Layered, Sidebar, Cascade, Scale, and Tape-line. These advanced types provide more flexibility for matching your brand and improving user experience with enhanced visual styles and additional placement options.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What are visibility settings?">
    Visibility settings (available on Professional and Premium plans) allow you to control when and where selectors and recommendation banners appear on your store. You can set conditions based on pages, customer behavior, or other criteria to show the right selector at the right time.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What is 'Automatic markets redirects'?">
    This feature automatically redirects visitors to their local Shopify Market based on their geolocation. For example, visitors from France are redirected to your French market, improving their shopping experience and increasing conversions.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What are custom resources?">
    Custom resources (Premium plan only) allow you to create switchers that link to external platforms, third-party websites, or custom URLs. This is useful for redirecting customers to sister brands on different platforms (like Magento), regional websites, or localized support pages. Learn more in our [Use Cases guide](/selecty/features/use-cases).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I connect multiple stores on Premium?">
    Yes! The Premium plan allows you to manage selectors for multiple Shopify stores from one dashboard. This is perfect for agencies managing client stores or brands with multiple storefronts.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I upgrade or downgrade anytime?">
    Yes! You can change plans at any time. Changes take effect immediately, and billing is prorated. Downgrading to Free retains your basic selectors but removes premium features.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How much do I save with yearly billing?">
    You save **25%** on paid plans with yearly billing:
    - Professional: $107.91/year (vs $143.88/year monthly)
    - Premium: $197.91/year (vs $263.88/year monthly)
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Do you offer a trial period?">
    Yes! Both Professional and Premium plans include a 7-day free trial. The Free plan is available permanently with no trial needed.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What payment methods do you accept?">
    All payments are processed through Shopify, so you can use any payment method accepted by Shopify.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
